	\section{Psalms 9}
	\label{sec:Ps}
\begin{enumerate}
\item To the Overseer, `On the Death of Labben.' --A Psalm of David.\marginpar{David was the second king of Israel, anointed by Prophet Samuel.} I confess, O Jehovah, with all my heart, I recount all Thy wonders,

\item I rejoice and exult in Thee, I praise Thy Name, O Most High.

\item In mine enemies turning backward, they stumble and perish from Thy face.

\item For Thou hast done my judgment and my right. Thou hast sat on a throne, A judge of righteousness.

\item Thou hast rebuked nations, Thou hast destroyed the wicked, Their name Thou hast blotted out to the age and for ever.

\item O thou Enemy, Finished have been destructions for ever, As to cities thou hast plucked up, Perished hath their memorial with them.

\item And Jehovah to the age abideth, He is preparing for judgment His throne.

\item And He judgeth the world in righteousness, He judgeth the peoples in uprightness.

\item And Jehovah is a tower for the bruised, A tower for times of adversity.

\item They trust in Thee who do know Thy name, For Thou hast not forsaken Those seeking Thee, O Jehovah.

\item Sing ye praise to Jehovah, inhabiting Zion, Declare ye among the peoples His acts,

\item For He who is seeking for blood Them hath remembered, He hath not forgotten the cry of the afflicted.

\item Favour me, O Jehovah, See mine affliction by those hating me, Thou who liftest me up from the gates of death,

\item So that I recount all Thy praise, In the gates of the daughter of Zion. I rejoice on Thy salvation.

\item Sunk have nations in a pit they made, In a net that they hid hath their foot been captured.

\item Jehovah hath been known, Judgment He hath done, By a work of his hands Hath the wicked been snared. Meditation. Selah.

\item The wicked do turn back to Sheol, All nations forgetting God.

\item For not for ever is the needy forgotten, The hope of the humble lost to the age.

\item Rise, O Jehovah, let not man be strong, Let nations be judged before Thy face.

\item Appoint, O Jehovah, a director to them, Let nations know they are men! Selah.

\end{enumerate}
